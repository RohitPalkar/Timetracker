## 1. Product Purpose

MyTracker is an enterprise work-management and time-tracking platform.

It provides a single platform for organizations to manage:

- Organizations and users
- Projects and sub-projects
- Teams and project memberships
- Agile delivery
- Sprints, epics, stories, tasks and bugs
- Timesheets and time tracking
- QA and testing
- Resource planning
- Reports and analytics
- Surveys
- Notifications
- Documents
- AI-assisted workspace
- Administration and settings

The platform must support both simple and structured project delivery.
